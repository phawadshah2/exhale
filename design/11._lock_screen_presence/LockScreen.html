Exhale on the Lock Screen

Goal: every time the phone is picked up, Exhale is in view. The numbers are today's count against today's limit, the countdown once the day's first log starts it, and the days left to the quit date. Example day: limit 8, 4 logged, next one on schedule at 10:13 AM, quit date in 41 days.

1 · Live Activity

Starts with each log and shows the countdown. iOS ticks the time itself. Fifteen minutes after the time is up it goes quiet (green bar, no clock), and the next log or an app open takes it away. iOS ends it after 8 hours anyway.

Always there, also when no countdown runs. Three sizes; people pick which to add. Before the day's first log they show the count and "8 left".

5 · "Log one" without opening the app

One action, offered by iOS everywhere: Siri ("Log one in Exhale"), Shortcuts, the Action Button, a Control Center button, a Lock Screen control and the widget's +. It saves with no trigger and confirms in the Island. The log waits in a queue, and the app adds it when it next opens.
