Paywall, no price given

Drawn in points at 1:1 with the app's sizes, then scaled down. The dashed line is the top of the button on the chat, the plan reveal and the welcome. "Now" is the current build; A, B and C are the options. Every option puts the button on the line, and the links take the fine print's slot.

What changes in every option
  • The button sits exactly where it does on the chat, plan and welcome pages. "Terms" and "Privacy" become one small line in the fine print's slot (14 pt tall, the same as the reveal's fine print), each with a 44 pt invisible tap area. Today they are 44 pt tall buttons, which lift the button 30 pt.
The options for the empty space
  • A. Flow from the top. The cards follow "What you get" straight away, and the spare space sits above the button. On the largest phones the gap moves rather than goes away.
  • B. Steps down the page, spaced evenly (recommended). When they fit, the three steps run down the page with their sentences, as on the priced paywall you approved, in place of the icon row. Any space left is shared out between the blocks. The cards stay just above the button, and small iPhones keep the icon row.
  • C. Story in the middle. The icon row and "What you get" are centred in the space above the cards, so the gap is split above and below them.
