Rules
  • The stat card, the three steps and the button follow the selected plan. The plan cards do not change.
  • Regular iPhones show the steps down the page; small iPhones show them as a row of icons.
  • No price given (or the price's currency differs from the App Store's): the icon row and "What you get" replace the stat card and the steps.
  • Trial already used: no "7 days free" tag, Annual's steps become charge / renewal / cancel, the headline becomes "Quitting is hard. Starting isn't."
  • App Store unreachable: the plan cards are replaced by the message and Try again; the button is disabled.
  • Nothing scrolls at the standard text size. With larger accessibility text the page scrolls and the button stays pinned.
  • Icons are stand-ins for Cupertino icons through AppIcons. "pods" follows the habit.
